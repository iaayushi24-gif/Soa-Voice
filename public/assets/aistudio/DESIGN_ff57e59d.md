## Brand & Style

The design system blends hyper-feminine Y2K Barbiecore with futuristic cyber-chic opulence. Crafted specifically for voice-activated nail artistry workflows and modern nail studio business management, the personality is vivacious, playfully luxurious, confident, and tactile.

The design movement synthesizes **Y2K Retro-Futurism, Cyber-Chic Glassmorphism, and Glossy Skeuomorphic Nuances**. The interface evokes the high-energy luxury of an elite Beverly Hills or Harajuku nail studio: gel coats, iridescent foil decals, mirror-chrome topcoats, and sparkling micro-animations. 

UI elements feature ultra-glossy translucent layers, chrome bevels, floating starburst sparkles (✦, ✧), and pill-form tactile interactive surfaces that react fluidly to voice inputs with visual soundwave ripples and iridescent halos.

## Colors

The palette embraces unabashed chromatic saturation balanced by crisp chrome and luminous milky tones:

- **Primary (`#FF1493` - Electric Magenta / Hyper Hot Pink)**: Leads high-priority interactions, active voice listening states, primary calls-to-action, and key metrics. Supported by vivid accents of `#FF459B`.
- **Secondary (`#E0B0FF` - Holographic Lilac)**: Powers soft gradients, voice processing waves, subheadings, and secondary interactive states, pairing seamlessly with deep plum undertones (`#DDA0DD`).
- **Tertiary (`#7DF9FF` - Cyber Baby Blue)**: Represents digital connectivity, AI listening prompts, and cool holographic reflections. Counterbalanced by joyful Buttercup Yellow (`#FFF07C`) for badges and ratings.
- **Neutral (`#FFF5F9` - Pearlescent Milk)**: A warm, blush-tinted milky canvas replacing stark sterile whites. Deep typography leans into rich berry-tinted obsidian (`#2A081B`) for striking contrast and legibility.
- **Accents & Finishes**: Linear mirror-chrome gradients (`linear-gradient(135deg, #FFFFFF 0%, #E2E8F0 50%, #CBD5E1 100%)`) with hot-pink ambient specular glints.

## Typography

The typographic strategy pairs flamboyant Y2K cyber-editorial display forms with ultra-clean, legible modern sans-serif foundations:

- **Headlines (`Syne`)**: Expressive, sculptural, and unapologetically bold. Delivers high-fashion Y2K attitude for salon service titles, client names, and prominent promotional displays.
- **Body (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Clean, rounded, highly legible humanist geometry. Ensures client appointment schedules, inventory tallies, and polish formula instructions remain effortless to digest in fast-paced salon environments.
- **Labels & Voice Prompts (`Space Grotesk`)**: Technical, futuristic Y2K monoline feel. Applied to speech recognition transcription tags, active audio state meters, timestamps, and currency markers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout utilizes a dynamic fluid grid system tailored for hands-free and touch interactions across busy studio stations:

- **Desktop & iPad Pro Stations (12-column grid)**: Designed with 2rem (`32px`) margins and 1.25rem (`20px`) gutters. Optimizes split-screen real estate: voice dictation console and booking book on the left, live inventory/swatch palette library on the right.
- **Mobile (4-column grid)**: Uses 1rem (`16px`) outer margins and 0.75rem (`12px`) gutters. Touch targets and voice buttons expand dynamically to support single-hand, thumb-driven operation while technicians work on clients.
- **Rhythm**: Element spacing scales strictly through multiples of 4px/8px, prioritizing generous touch clearance around voice trigger surfaces.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy abandons traditional flat shadows in favor of **Glossy Glassmorphic Luminance and Ambient Iridescent Halos**:

- **Tier 0 (Base Canvas)**: Soft, pearlescent milk backdrop (`#FFF5F9`) with faint blurred ambient orbs of electric pink and soft buttercup yellow drifting in the background.
- **Tier 1 (Surface Cards & Panels)**: Frosted neon glass with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`, `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.72)` fill, encased in a 1px metallic chrome gradient border (`#FFFFFF` down to `#FFB6D9`). Casts an ambient drop shadow: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(255, 20, 147, 0.12)`.
- **Tier 2 (Interactive Floating Pill Surfaces)**: Specular gloss highlights layered over bubblegum fills. Casts a dual-layered shadow: a crisp direct shadow `0 4px 12px rgba(255, 20, 147, 0.25)` and a wide, diffuse glow `0 12px 28px rgba(224, 176, 255, 0.35)`.
- **Tier 3 (Active Voice Listening HUD & Modals)**: Full chrome and holographic rim lighting. Surrounded by pulsating animated ring glows radiating outward in `#FF1493` and `#7DF9FF`.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary revolves around **Pill-Shaped (Curvature 3)** silhouettes and bubble geometry. 

Elements adopt continuous, ultra-smooth radiuses evoking glossy gel nail tips, polished Y2K flip-phone curves, and stickers. Full pill shapes (`border-radius: 9999px`) dominate buttons, search bars, transcription status tags, and chips. Cards and dialogs embrace plush rounded envelopes of 2rem to 3rem (`32px` to `48px`). Decorative Y2K starburst cutouts and metallic heart icons punctuate corner anchors and tab bars.

## Components

- **Voice Command Orb & Action Button**:
  A floating 64px–80px pill or circular trigger finished in mirror-chrome with a vibrant hot-pink gel center. While listening, the orb pulses with an iridescent multi-color wave (`#FF1493` to `#7DF9FF`) and emits dancing starburst particles (✦). Voice transcripts appear in real-time above the orb inside a translucent baby-pink frosted speech bubble.

- **Buttons**:
  Hyper-tactile pill buttons featuring a top inner specular gloss highlight (`inset 0 1px 1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`). 
  - *Primary*: Hot Pink gradient fill (`linear-gradient(180deg, #FF459B 0%, #FF1493 100%)`) with crisp white Syne typography.
  - *Secondary*: Cyber chrome-silver surface with a hot-pink border and subtle lilac drop shadow.
  - *Interactive Feedback*: On press, buttons compress subtly (`scale(0.97)`) with an iridescent glimmer flash.

- **Cards (Frosted Neon Panels)**:
  Used for booking slots, client profiles, and nail art recipes. Built with translucent milky-white backdrops, chrome hairline rims, and upper-right Y2K starburst accents (✧). Cards feature playful sticker-like status badges in buttercup yellow and baby blue.

- **Chips & Tag Badges**:
  Pill-shaped chips with glossy liquid-gel fills. Used for nail shape tags (Stiletto, Coffin, Almond), polish finishes (Chrome, Velvet, Cat-Eye), and voice status markers. Selected chips gain a neon magenta rim and a tiny sparkling diamond motif.

- **Input Fields & Voice Dictation Prompts**:
  Generous pill-shaped inputs (`52px` height) with pale lavender-pink translucent backgrounds. Inactive states show placeholder text in soft plum (`#A37096`). Active voice focus triggers a glowing `#FF1493` outer rim accompanied by an animated audio frequency wave icon.

- **Checkboxes & Toggles**:
  - *Toggles*: Miniature pill capsules; the thumb is a reflective chrome sphere that glides smoothly into a neon hot-pink active track.
  - *Checkboxes*: Soft rounded-square or heart-shaped enclosures that fill with vibrant bubblegum pink and a sparkling chrome checkmark upon selection.

- **Custom Nail Studio Components**:
  - *Voice-Driven Shade Selector*: Horizontally scrolling carousel of high-gloss gel swatches framed in silver bezels, dynamically selectable via voice prompts (e.g., "Select Bubblegum Glaze").
  - *Sparkle Meter / Tip Calculator*: Tactile slider bar designed with an iridescent liquid fill and chrome heart thumb.
